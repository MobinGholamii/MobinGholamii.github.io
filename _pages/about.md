I am a physician and researcher working at the intersection of **artificial intelligence, clinical medicine, and occupational health**. I am currently a PhD student in **Artificial Intelligence in Health** at Iran University of Medical Sciences and an **MPH** student at Shiraz University of Medical Sciences.


Research interests
======
- **Uncertainty quantification and conformal prediction** for medical imaging and clinical risk models — making AI predictions come with reliable, calibrated guarantees, especially for imbalanced and ordinal outcomes such as diabetic retinopathy grading.
- **Clinical prediction models**, including 30-day readmission after acute heart failure using MIMIC-IV and local hospital data.
- **AI and NLP for trauma research**, in collaboration on traumatic spinal cord injury.
- **Occupational and workforce health**: large-scale occupational examination data, metabolic health in industrial workers, and burnout and quiet quitting among healthcare professionals.
